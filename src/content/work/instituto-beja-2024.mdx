<CaseSummary>
  <CaseSummaryItem title="The problem">
    Instituto Beja's annual report brings together governance, research, partnerships and a broad
    portfolio of philanthropic initiatives. The digital edition needed to make that volume of
    information approachable without losing its editorial character.
  </CaseSummaryItem>

  <CaseSummaryItem title="What I built">
    A responsive, bilingual report that presents the institute's 2024 work as a continuous web
    narrative, combining section-based navigation, expressive motion and reusable content patterns.
  </CaseSummaryItem>
</CaseSummary>

<CaseDetail
  title="A long report that still feels navigable"
  code="Section navigation, bilingual content and progressively loaded chapters"
>
  The experience divides a large editorial document into clear chapters, from the founder's letter
  and rebrand to strategic pillars, supported projects and the final balance. Persistent navigation
  and Portuguese/English content help readers move through the report without breaking its visual
  rhythm.
</CaseDetail>
